- 실제 설정: model seedance_2_5, mode omni_reference, duration 20, aspect_ratio 1:1, resolution 1080p, generate_audio false, bitrate_mode high, count 1임. 본춤 17초 전체와 앞뒤 정면 연결 1.5초씩을 하나의 영상으로 요청함.
- 입력은 같은 정면 기준 2차 이미지의 start_image와 end_image 및 카메라 보정 정사각형 참조 17초의 video임. 이미지 ID는 a42ebe5c-2df0-4ea1-bff7-39af5ad352d8, 영상 ID는 4f8372a5-2747-4115-8c1f-e096f59c952a임. 다른 춤 영상이나 문서는 입력하지 않음.
- 사전 견적 320크레딧을 안내한 뒤 출력 1개만 제출함. 작업 ID는 34e9e908-dc1b-4c41-99bc-76c6b689c592임. pending 응답과 실제 제출 설정 확인함. 실제 청구 내역을 대조한 금액은 아님.
- 제출 프롬프트:

```text
Create one continuous 20-second square 1:1 full-body illustrated character animation on a perfectly uniform solid chroma green background. The supplied start and end image is the ONLY source for character appearance, costume, neutral frontal standing pose, scale and framing. The supplied 17-second video is the ONLY source for dance motion. It is the user's complete dance reference, spatially corrected to reduce the original camera zoom and tracking; ignore its rectangular image edges and gray padding. Transfer only the masked performer's body and limb motions, never its background or camera movement. Timing: 0.0-1.5 seconds, begin in the supplied neutral frontal standing pose and move naturally into the reference video's initial pose. 1.5-18.5 seconds, reproduce ALL 17 seconds of the reference dance ONCE, in original order at original speed. Do not omit, condense, loop or repeat any section and do not invent a new dance. 18.5-20.0 seconds, naturally settle from the final reference pose back to exactly the supplied frontal neutral standing pose with both arms relaxed down. Do not replace any of the 17-second dance with these connections. Keep the camera locked in one frontal position with constant lens and scale throughout: no zoom, pan, tilt, tracking, shake, cuts or changes of framing. Preserve the character size of the still image, about 60 percent of the square height, with generous green clearance. The complete hat ornament, both hands and both shoes must stay fully visible inside the canvas for every frame. Preserve the same illustrated mask, hat with red brim and white ornament, black head and shoulder drape, plain ivory sleeves and trousers, red WAIST sash and waist panels and straw shoes. Red cloth attaches only at the waist; no wrist ribbons, new props or redesign. Solid green #00FF00 edge to edge, without scenery, floor, shadows, gradients, text, logos, borders, fades or visual transitions. Silent video.
```
